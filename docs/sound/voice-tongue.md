# The tongue: what Relic's people sound like

Kyle, 2026-11-05: "I like it being a Germanic inspired but fake language with real voices." The
recorded voices speak a made-up tongue that sounds old and Germanic, a little Old English: real,
natural human voices with a consistent feel, grounded, never cartoonish. It carries tone only. The
words on screen stay the game's own English lines, and **the tongue never appears on screen.**

## Sounds

- **Consonants:** hard and close to the front of the mouth: k, g, t, d, b, h, r (rolled or tapped),
  l, m, n, s, v, w, and the throat sound ch (as in loch). Doubled consonants for weight: kk, rr, tt,
  mm, ss.
- **Vowels:** a, e, i, o, u, and the round ones ä, ö, ü. Diphthongs ai, au, ei.
- **Endings:** -en, -ung, -och, -ach, -ar, -eth, -ren, -um, -ek, -mach. A hyphen joins a word and an
  ending for a little more push ("tovach-en", "kraudt-ar").
- **Shape:** two-syllable words, stress on the first; a line is one to three short phrases.

**Checked against:** German, Dutch, English, the Scandinavian tongues, and the rude words of the
languages Kyle's friends might know. Left out on purpose: real words (ach, ja, nein, heim, gott, mein,
dank, kom, hallo, gut), anything near heil, sieg or kampf, and words that are real somewhere else
(tummen, orren, rukke and trakk are Swedish or Norwegian and were changed). No line borrows a slur or a
swear in any language we checked.

## Heroes

Heroes don't speak the tongue in a fight: they make the sounds people make. Each hero voice has:

- **a grunt** on a heavy swing: "Hhrah!"
- **a gasp** when hurt: "Hhh!"
- **a cry** when downed: "Aaagh..."
- **a breath** on getting up: "Hhhuh."
- **a laugh** for the idles (the Ranger knocked flat by the hound): "Hah hah hah!"

A Cleric's voice also prays, murmured on a heal ("Ölmeth saivren." / "Saivren, kesswen.") and said
hard on a smite ("Kraudt ölmeth!" / "Ölmeth, kraudt-ar!").
