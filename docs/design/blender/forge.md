# Blender brief: the forge

Read [README.md](README.md) first (scale, axes, texture rules, export).

**What it is.** The smithy (the forge you go in to make and mend), on Smiths' Lane in the new layout, glowing on
your right as you walk west. The smith works at the anvil out front (the anvil, quench tub and sacks are props in
the street already; leave room for them).

**Footprint today.** Block 7 wide by 4 deep (town.ts: x 31, y 3); door spot at the middle of the south front.

**Shape.**
- Stone, two storeys and a gable, about 6 tiles to the ridge; not as tall as the hall or church, but broad and heavy.
- A huge open workshop bay in the ground floor front (about 3 wide and 2.4 high) under a heavy timber lintel: the
  forge hearth inside glowing, an anvil, tools racked on the back wall. This bay is the way in.
- A tall square stone chimney at one side (about 1.2 square), rising to 9 tiles, a little smoke stain at the top.
- A timber and plaster upper storey with one or two shuttered windows; a lean-to with a slate roof on the other side.
- The anvil sign on an iron bracket (the kit's `sign-anvil.png`).

**Glow.** The hearth and the whole bay's inside (strong, orange), the upper windows faintly.

**Takes to look at.** `land-forge-0..3.png`; take 2 has the clearest bay and chimney.
