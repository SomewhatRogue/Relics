# Blender brief: the Drowned Rat

Read [README.md](README.md) first (scale, axes, texture rules, export).

**What it is.** Wickhold's tavern, low in town by the sewer grate, rambling and warm. In the new layout it sits at
the foot of the Rat Steps and is turned to front north, up the steps (the game turns it; model it facing south).

**Footprint today.** Block 8 wide by 4 deep (town.ts: x 13, y 30); door spot at the middle of the front.

**Shape.**
- Three storeys, each jettied further out over the one below (about 0.3 tiles a storey), about 9 tiles to the
  ridge; a crooked, added-onto feel: one end a storey lower, a chimney or two leaning.
- Ground floor stone: a wide double door (about 1.6 wide) under a lantern either side, small lit windows, barrels
  and a bench by the door.
- A balcony with a railing across the first floor.
- Upper floors timber and plaster, many small leaded windows, two or three gables to the front with dormers.
- The tankard sign on an iron bracket by the door (the kit's `sign-tankard.png` can be its texture).
- Slate roof, patched, mossy.

**Glow.** Almost every window lit, the door's lanterns, firelight in the doorway.

**Takes to look at.** `land-tavern-0..3.png`; take 2 has the clearest balcony.
