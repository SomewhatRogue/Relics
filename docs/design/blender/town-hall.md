# Blender brief: the town hall

Read [README.md](README.md) first (scale, axes, texture rules, export).

**What it is.** Where the council sits and the map table is (Lantern Square's slices). On the square's north side
in the new layout, its back to Kirkhill, seen behind the Lantern from the Rat Steps.

**Footprint today.** Block 6 wide by 4 deep (town.ts: x 15, y 3); door spot at the middle of the south front. In
the picture it's wider than that; the model may run to 7 wide if the layout gives it room.

**Shape.**
- Three storeys, about 9 to 10 tiles to the top of the clock gable.
- Ground floor: a stone arcade of three round arches (the middle one the door, double, about 1.4 wide), broad steps
  in front the whole width.
- First and second floors: timber and plaster, jettied a little over the arcade, rows of leaded windows, three tall
  hanging banners between them (dark green and rust, gold device).
- A central gable with a round clock face (about 1.2 across), a small bell turret with a flag above the ridge;
  dormers in the roof either side.
- Slate roof, hipped at the ends.

**Glow.** The windows (many, warm), lanterns either side of the arches, the clock face faintly.
