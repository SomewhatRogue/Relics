# Blender brief: the church

Read [README.md](README.md) first (scale, axes, texture rules, export).

**What it is.** Wickhold's church, Mother Isolde's: the tallest thing in town, seen from everywhere. In the new
layout it stands on Kirkhill at the head of the Kirk Steps, its front looking down them; the town's money shot is
coming up the Rat Steps with the Lantern before you, the town hall behind it and the church above.

**Footprint today.** Block 8 wide by 4.5 deep (town.ts: x 3, y 3); door spot at the middle of the south front. The
model may be deeper than the block only if the layout gives it room; ask the layout chat.

**Shape.**
- A nave 6 to 7 tiles wide, its gable front about 7 tiles to the ridge, running back the block's depth.
- A square bell tower over the front, about 2.8 tiles square, rising to 12 to 14 tiles, an open belfry (two
  pointed openings a side, a bell in each) and a parapet or short spire with a cross at the top.
- The west front: a deep pointed doorway (double doors, about 1.4 wide and 2.2 high) up three or four broad steps,
  a big rose window over it (about 2.4 across), two buttresses with pinnacles either side, a lantern on each.
- Down the nave's sides: buttresses every 1.5 tiles with tall narrow pointed windows between (the kit's
  `side-nave-<n>.png` is the look).
- Slate roof, steep, mossy at the eaves.

**Glow.** The rose window, the lancet windows, the door's lanterns, a faint light in the belfry.

**Takes to look at.** `land-church-0..3.png`; take 1 has the clearest tower.
