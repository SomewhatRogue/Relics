# Titles: a name earned on the church's road, or the hungry one

Draft, 2026-11-23 (Claude Opus 5.5), from Kyle's calls in the Holy and hungry chat. Names are the lore keeper's
drafts, for Kyle's yes.

## Kyle's calls

- A title goes before or around your name, chosen from the ones you've earned (Kyle, 2026-11-23: "the holy or the
  shadow or the greedy etc").
- **Cosmetic only.** No stats, no gifts.
- **Earned by deeds, then bought:** once you've done enough at the church, or under it, you can buy that road's
  titles (Kyle: "you get titles by buying them once you've done enough things at either the church or the under
  church").
- **Both roads:** a hero can earn and own titles from both, but **can't wear one while on the other road**.
- **Shown to everyone,** online too.
- **Pretty expensive; a flex.** Easy ones at low level.

## How it works (drafts)

- **The two roads.**
  - The church's (Father Odric sells them, for a gift at the Lantern, as his shelf is paid): worn only while clean.
  - The Keeping Room's (Dorcas sells them, for coin): worn only while corrupt.
  - A worn title that doesn't fit the road you're on now shows nothing until you're back on it; it isn't lost.
- **Deeds counted** (from what's already built):
  - Church: hungry pieces given up; amends steps reached; ripe vowed pieces given up at the crossroads; gifts at the
    Lantern (one deed a gold's worth, say).
  - Under: relics fed at the trough; vowed pieces given to Dorcas; vowed pieces let go bad at the crossroads; jobs
    finished in hungry gear (one deed for every five).
- **Four titles a road,** each needing more deeds and costing more. Prices in jobs' pay at the hero's level (the
  economy's measure, docs/design/economy.md), so they follow its tuning:

| Step | Deeds on that road | Price | For |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First | 1 | about 2 jobs | anyone, early: easy at low level |
| Second | 5 | about an evening (6 jobs) | |
| Third | 15 | a couple of evenings (15 jobs) | |
| Fourth | 40 | weeks (60 jobs) | the flex |

- **Names** (the lore keeper's, 2026-11-23, Kyle's three among them; for his yes), easiest first:
  - Church: THE GIVEN (after: TESS THE GIVEN), VOWED (before: VOWED TESS), THE STEADFAST (after), THE HOLY (after,
    the flex: the church's own word for its best; nothing says it's true, so the Lantern's secret holds).
  - Under: THE GREEDY (after), THE KEPT (after; THE HOARDER if "kept" reads wrong), SHADOW (before: SHADOW TESS),
    GILDED (before, the flex: wears the keeping like jewellery). GIVEN and KEPT mirror the bible's "every coin is
    either kept or given".
- **Where it shows:** over the hero's head with their name, in the party list, on Best in Wickhold. Before or after
  the name, by title ("SHADOW TESS", "TESS THE GREEDY").
- **Choosing:** at Odric's or Dorcas's, a TITLES button: the road's four, which are earned, which bought, and wear one
  (or none). Saved on the profile.
