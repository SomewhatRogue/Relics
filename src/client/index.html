WARRIOR

The training room

The goblin butcher winds up before every heavy blow. Its red shape on the floor fills, then it lands.

  • Attack click or tap. Keep pressing for the weapon's combo. Hold to charge a heavy blow; release to swing.
  • Block hold right click (or BLOCK). Costs stamina; run out and your guard breaks.
  • Parry tap block when the red telegraph flashes white. No damage, the goblin is stunned, and your next hit is a counter for 2.5x.
  • Dodge Space (or ROLL). Untouchable mid-dodge. Striped circles can't be blocked: dodge out. Each kit dodges its own way.
  • Class C (or CLASS) for the demo: Warrior (sword and shield, greatsword), Ranger (longbow, spear), Rogue (twin daggers), Mage (focus staff), Healer (mace and censer). Q (or WEAPON) swaps within the class.
  • Ranger taps loose arrows; hold to draw a shot that pierces every goblin in a line and knocks them into walls. Mage taps sparks; hold for an orb that bursts; the staff casts with your equipped relic's element; dodge is a blink. Healer every third blow and the held blessing heal everyone near you.
  • Relic E (or RELIC). The javelin pins small goblins to walls and sticks in big ones. The grappling hook yanks small goblins to you, or pulls you to walls, pillars and big ones. The gale horn blasts goblins away by their weight. The blast flask lobs over heads and bursts where it lands (where you point on a laptop). All recharge. L (or RELICS) takes another rack of three.
  • Augments T (or AUGMENT) fits the equipped relic with fire, frost or storm. Fire burns and spreads by touch. Frost chills; frost again freezes solid, and a heavy blow or a wall shatters the ice. Storm arcs to nearby goblins and to any javelin stuck in one. Fire meets frost: steam stuns everything near. Storm on the burning: a flare throws them.
  • Kits: sword and shield blocks best (wide guard, easiest parry, roll); greatsword hits hardest but parries need tight timing and its roll is heavy; spear keeps its distance with a backstep that keeps you facing; twin daggers are fast with a long dash and short recharge, poor at blocking.
  • Swap Q for weapon (sword and shield, greatsword, spear, twin daggers). Relics: hold RELIC on a phone, slide onto one and let go; 1, 2, 3 or R on a laptop. You carry up to 3.
  • From behind every blow does more. Twin daggers (the rogue's kit) do 2.5x from behind: roll past a goblin and stab its back. Goblins turn slowly; the purple arc marks their back.
  • Move WASD, or the stick on the left of the screen.

The dummy on the left takes hits. The one on the right swings back slowly: practise parries on it.
