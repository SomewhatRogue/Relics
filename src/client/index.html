WARRIOR

Relic

You start in Wickhold, the last walled town. Walk to the job board in Lantern Square to take a job: three rooms (four for the story mission) in a village, a farm, the fields, a forest or a mine. One job a day is a deep job: five rooms, worse goblins, a relic at the bottom. Clear each room to open the way out at the top; the last room holds the job (free the caged, kill the named butcher, smash the larder). Three downs and you're driven back. Gold, experience and the people you save come home with you. Walk up into any door to go in and talk to whoever is there: the trainer for your class (perks), the squire in the barracks (the yard), the smith and the relic-wright at the forge (elements, and which relic you carry), the priest at the church, the families on Rescue Row. Out on a job, cottages and barns can be entered, and wooden buildings smashed or burned down. When the chapter's goals are met the council in the town hall decides what the town does next, and the town changes. Progress saves in this browser.

The fight

The goblin butcher winds up before every heavy blow. Its red shape on the floor fills, then it lands.

  • Attack click or tap. Keep pressing for the weapon's combo. Hold to charge a heavy blow; release to swing.
  • Block hold right click (or BLOCK). Costs stamina; run out and your guard breaks.
  • Parry tap block when the red telegraph flashes white. No damage, the goblin is stunned, and your next hit is a counter for 2.5x.
  • Dodge Space (or ROLL). Untouchable mid-dodge. Striped circles can't be blocked: dodge out. Each kit dodges its own way.
  • The wood a new hero wakes in Greyhollow the day after the column fell, and Hew teaches one move at a time: attack, dodge, block, parry, then your skill. A second hero can skip it (BEGIN IN WICKHOLD); WOOD on the start screen plays it again.
  • Your hero is made once, on the start screen: the class is for life (make another hero, up to three, to play another). Only the Rogue is quick on its feet; everyone else trades speed for something. Everyone starts with one weapon; others of your class drop from goblins or come from Halvard at the forge, and you swap them in your pack.
  • Gold spills where goblins die. Walk over it and the party splits it evenly. Driven back, you keep half.
  • Potions three on your belt; Q (or tap POTION) uses the front one. Sister's Bitter heals, Wall Brandy brings breath back, Lamp Oil and Frost Flasks are thrown where you aim, Smoke hides you. Goblins drop them; Agne, Gannet, Moll and Ivo sell them. Reorder the belt in your pack.
  • Chests USE beside one (F on a laptop) and stay put while it opens. Light leaks from the seams in the colour of the best thing inside. Everyone in the room gets their own roll, seen only by them. The last room of every job has one; a deep job has a chained chest that opens once the named butcher is dead.
  • Hungry gear (red) is the best there is, and every piece costs you something; the card says what. It can't be dropped, melted or upgraded. Leave it on the church step to be rid of it.
  • Pack I (or tap PACK), any time: what's in hand, your armor and two rings, your skill, belt and relic, your spares, and every number as you stand. The world keeps going while it's open.
  • Gear comes in tiers by colour: Plain (white), Fine (green), Forged (blue), Tolmouth (purple). A weapon's type is how it attacks; its modifiers are how well. Armor is light, medium or heavy (how you move and how much you take); rings carry modifiers. Every card shows what it would change against what you wear, green better, red worse. Halvard sells Fine weapons and armor, Dessa Forged weapons and rings. At Halvard's anvil, melt spare gear for steel and level a piece up (white to +2, green +3, blue +4, purple +5) with gold and steel; Ivo rerolls a modifier on what you wear for 40 gold.
  • Every class has a weapon, a guard, a dodge and one skill of its own (E, or tap SKILL). Nobody starts with a relic.
  • Warrior sword and shield (later a greatsword or spear); the best guard; lifts and throws. Skill: War Cry (nearby allies catch their breath and block at half cost; small goblins flinch).
  • Ranger a short sword on a tap, a bow on a hold: the longbow, and later a shortbow or crossbow. The dodge is a jump back that leaves a snare where you stood (one every few seconds). A hound runs at your heel and now and then goes for what you're hitting; it can't be killed, but a butcher swinging close by scares it off for a while. Skill: Hunter's Net (pins small goblins flat, slows big ones).
  • Rogue twin daggers, 2.5x from behind, the long dash, the quickest feet. Skill: Shadowstep (straight behind the goblin you face).
  • Mage spells by cast time: wand sparks, and later a staff orb that bursts wide or a rod lance through a line. Skill: Lodestone (drags goblins together, then bursts).
  • Healer every third mace blow heals everyone near; hold for a holy smite that stuns. Coals: a dead butcher leaves a live coal for a little while; a Healer who walks over it feeds the censer: stronger heals, for good, and the hungry hunt that Healer first. Skill: Sanctuary (blessed ground that mends allies and burns goblins, and a ward on whoever is most hurt that soaks blows and throws arrows back). Good on your own too.
  • Relics are pried out of named butchers. You leave Greyhollow with the javelin. After that, one waits at the bottom of each day's deep job, five rooms down: the grappling hook is next (a small goblin comes to you; anything heavier, or a wall, pulls you to it; only a Warrior or a Healer can haul in the big ones). Carry one at a time: R, or tap RELIC. Change which at the relic-wright.
  • Elements the relic-wright at the forge fits your skill or a relic with fire, frost or storm. A Mage's spells take the element of whatever is readied. Fire burns and spreads by touch. Frost chills; frost again freezes solid, and a heavy blow or a wall shatters the ice. Storm arcs to nearby goblins and to any javelin stuck in one. Fire meets frost: steam stuns everything near. Storm on the burning: a flare throws them.
  • The squire by the swing dummy keeps getting hit. Heal him, bless him, rally him.
  • Grab G (or GRAB, which shows up when you can): lift a small goblin you've stunned, netted, frozen, staggered or got behind. Then attack to slam it into the ground, or grab again to throw it at another goblin. It wriggles free after 3 s. A Warrior can also lift barrels, crates, the table and their broken pieces: throw them (big ones break on what they hit) or slam them down. Anyone can smash them; the oil barrel bursts into flame.
  • Skill E (or tap SKILL). Relic R (or tap RELIC), once you've won one. Each recharges.
  • From behind every blow does more. Twin daggers (the rogue's kit) do 2.5x from behind: roll past a goblin and stab its back. Goblins turn slowly; the purple arc marks their back.
  • Heights some rooms have a ledge along the back, with goblin archers on it (a thin line on the floor is an arrow coming: block, parry or roll). Go up by the stairs; a blade only reaches your own level, but arrows and spells reach up and down, and shots from up top hit harder. Knock a goblin off the edge and it takes the fall.
  • Forks halfway through a job the road splits: the high road (archers above, fewer goblins below) or the low road (more goblins, someone caged). The way on opens when the whole party stands in it.
  • Sound SND switches all sound; the note switches the music. Every place has its own music, and drums rise when goblins come for you.
  • Move WASD, or the stick on the left of the screen.

The dummy on the left takes hits. The one on the right swings back slowly: practise parries on it.
