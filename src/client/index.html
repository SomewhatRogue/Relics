WARRIOR

Relic

You start in Wickhold, the last walled town. Walk to the job board in Lantern Square to take a job: three rooms (four for the story mission) in a village, a farm, the fields, a forest or a mine. Clear each room to open the way out at the top; the last room holds the job (free the caged, kill the named butcher, smash the larder). Three downs and you're driven back. Gold, experience and the people you save come home: walk up into any door to go in and talk to whoever is there: the trainer (perks) and his squire (the yard) in the training hall, the smith and the relic-wright at the forge, the priest at the church, the families on Rescue Row. Out on a job, cottages and barns can be entered, and wooden buildings smashed or burned down. When the chapter's goals are met the council in the town hall decides what the town does next, and the town changes. Progress saves in this browser.

The fight

The goblin butcher winds up before every heavy blow. Its red shape on the floor fills, then it lands.

  • Attack click or tap. Keep pressing for the weapon's combo. Hold to charge a heavy blow; release to swing.
  • Block hold right click (or BLOCK). Costs stamina; run out and your guard breaks.
  • Parry tap block when the red telegraph flashes white. No damage, the goblin is stunned, and your next hit is a counter for 2.5x.
  • Dodge Space (or ROLL). Untouchable mid-dodge. Striped circles can't be blocked: dodge out. Each kit dodges its own way.
  • Your hero is made once, on the start screen: the class is for life (make another hero, up to three, to play another). Only the Rogue is quick on its feet; everyone else trades speed for something. The smith at the forge changes your kit within your class; the relic-wright fits elements.
  • Warrior sword and shield, greatsword or spear. Relics: javelin, grappling hook, war horn.
  • Ranger one arrow per press, and the draw is everything: the longbow takes 1.4 s to a shot that pierces a whole line; the shortbow draws fast and lets you walk; the crossbow fires the moment you press, then a long reload. Relics: hunter's net (snares small goblins flat), grappling hook.
  • Rogue twin daggers, 2.5x from behind, the long dash. Relics: shadowstep (straight behind the goblin you face), grappling hook.
  • Mage spells by cast time: the wand flicks sparks, the staff gathers 1.6 s for an orb that bursts wide, the rod 1 s for a lance through a line. Spells take your relic's element. The blink is far but slow to come back. Relic: lodestone (lobbed; drags goblins together, then bursts).
  • Healer every third mace blow heals everyone near; hold to call a holy smite down on the goblin you face (big, and it stuns). Relics: blessing (heals an ally and makes them hit harder and take less; points at the most hurt ally, or where you aim on a laptop) and the war horn.
  • War horn every ally in earshot gets their stamina back, then twice as fast, and blocking costs half for 6 s.
  • Augments the relic-wright at the forge fits a relic with fire, frost or storm. Fire burns and spreads by touch. Frost chills; frost again freezes solid, and a heavy blow or a wall shatters the ice. Storm arcs to nearby goblins and to any javelin stuck in one. Fire meets frost: steam stuns everything near. Storm on the burning: a flare throws them.
  • The squire by the swing dummy keeps getting hit. Heal him, bless him, rally him.
  • Grab G (or GRAB, which shows up when you can): lift a small goblin you've stunned, netted, frozen, staggered or got behind. Then attack to slam it into the ground, or grab again to throw it at another goblin. It wriggles free after 3 s. A Warrior can also lift barrels, crates, the table and their broken pieces: throw them (big ones break on what they hit) or slam them down. Anyone can smash them; the oil barrel bursts into flame.
  • Relic E (or tap RELIC). On a phone, hold RELIC, slide onto another and let go to switch; 1, 2, 3 on a laptop. Each recharges.
  • From behind every blow does more. Twin daggers (the rogue's kit) do 2.5x from behind: roll past a goblin and stab its back. Goblins turn slowly; the purple arc marks their back.
  • Move WASD, or the stick on the left of the screen.

The dummy on the left takes hits. The one on the right swings back slowly: practise parries on it.
