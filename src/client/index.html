WARRIOR

The training room

The goblin butcher winds up before every heavy blow. Its red shape on the floor fills, then it lands.

  • Attack click or tap. Keep pressing for the weapon's combo. Hold to charge a heavy blow; release to swing.
  • Block hold right click (or BLOCK). Costs stamina; run out and your guard breaks.
  • Parry tap block when the red telegraph flashes white. No damage, the goblin is stunned, and your next hit is a counter for 2.5x.
  • Roll Space (or ROLL). Untouchable mid-roll. Striped circles can't be blocked: roll out.
  • Relic E (or RELIC). The javelin pins small goblins to walls and sticks in big ones. The grappling hook yanks small goblins to you, or pulls you to walls, pillars and big ones. Both recharge.
  • Swap Q for weapon (sword and shield, greatsword, spear), R for relic.
  • Move WASD, or the stick on the left of the screen.

The dummy on the left takes hits. The one on the right swings back slowly: practise parries on it.
